About Our Farm

Located in the heart of Nakasongola kalongo, Zziwa Farms is dedicated to producing high-quality, fresh fruits and livestock. Our pineapples and mangoes are naturally sweet, and our goats are raised with care and love.

Our Products

Pineapples

Golden and juicy pineapples grown with zero chemicals.

Mangoes

Sweet and ripe mangoes picked fresh from our trees.

Goats

Healthy goats ideal for milk, meat, or breeding.

Other Produce

We also grow bananas, cassava, and raise chickens.
